VOICE LOCK + AUTOMATIONS
========================
Run Install_Jarvis.bat again to add these (it keeps your key and memory).
The first time you use the voice lock it downloads a 26 MB voice model.

VOICE LOCK - Jarvis only listens to YOU
-------------------------------------
  "Learn my voice."            Jarvis asks you to keep talking for ~20 seconds
                               (reading something aloud works well), then it is ON.
  "Turn off the voice lock."   /  "Turn on the voice lock."
  "It keeps ignoring me."      -> say "make the voice lock less strict" or enrol again
                                  in the room you normally use.
  "It's letting others in."    -> "make the voice lock stricter".
  "Forget my voice."           deletes the saved voice print.

Good to know
  * It adds about a second before Jarvis starts hearing a command - it needs a
    moment of your speech to be sure it is you.
  * It matches voices. It is NOT a security lock: a recording of you, or a very
    similar voice, can get through, and you can be turned away on a one-word
    command or in a noisy room.
  * Speech it rejects is never sent to the cloud.
  * Only a voice print (256 numbers) is saved, not your recording.

AUTOMATIONS - standing rules, no need to ask each time (Windows)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  "Close Discord whenever I open it."
  "Block YouTube."                      (closes that browser tab, not the whole browser)
  "Focus mode for an hour - only Word and Chrome."
  "Take notes of everything I say."     /  "Stop the notes."  /  "Open my notes."
  "Tell me if I'm scrolling Instagram."   (looks at your screen every 30 s)
  "What rules do I have?"   "Pause all rules."   "Remove the Discord rule."

How it behaves
  * Closing is polite - like clicking the X - so an app with unsaved work asks
    you to save instead of losing it. Only if you say "force" does it escalate.
  * Apps already open when a rule starts are left alone; newly opened ones are
    closed. It cannot stop an app launching - it closes it about a second or two
    later.
  * Jarvis itself, Explorer, the taskbar and Task Manager are never touched, so a
    rule can never lock you out.
  * Notes go to Documents\Jarvis Notes. While notes run, Jarvis's voice is muted
    and it does not act on what it overhears. Say "stop the notes" to bring it
    back. Notes use whatever Gemini hears, so they are as accurate as its
    transcription. With the voice lock on, only your voice is written down unless
    you say "record everyone".
  * "Watch the screen" rules send a small screenshot to Gemini every few seconds
    while they are on. Jarvis tells you that when you set one up.
  * Rules are saved and come back when Jarvis starts.
