LINKING YOUR PHONE TO JARVIS
==============================

ANDROID phone  ->  Jarvis can use your phone from the PC
--------------------------------------------------------
Needs: the phone and the PC on the SAME Wi-Fi.

One-time, on the phone:
 1. Settings > About phone > tap "Build number" 7 times (turns on Developer options).
 2. Settings > System > Developer options > turn on "Wireless debugging".
    (Some phones: Settings > Developer options > Wireless debugging.)
 3. Tap "Wireless debugging" itself, then "Pair device with pairing code".
    You will see a 6-digit code and an address like 192.168.1.20:37045.
    Keep that screen open.

Then say to Jarvis:
    "Set up my phone."                       (first time only: downloads the phone tools)
    "Link my phone. The pairing address is 192.168.1.20:37045 and the code is 123456.
     Then connect to 192.168.1.20:41233."
The second address is the one on the main Wireless debugging screen (different port).
Jarvis remembers it and reconnects by itself next time. The port can change after the
phone restarts - then you just give the new address.

Prefer a cable? Turn on "USB debugging" in Developer options, plug the phone in, tap
Allow on the phone, and say "link my phone over Wi-Fi" - it switches to wireless.

Things you can say afterwards
    "What notifications do I have on my phone?"
    "Open WhatsApp on my phone."          "Look at my phone screen - what is on it?"
    "Send my last photo to this PC."      "Send this file to my phone: <path>"
    "On my phone, change the wallpaper to something dark."   (multi-step: runs on its own)
    "Call Mum from my phone."             (you press CONFIRM on the PC first)
    "Text +91... that I'm late."          (opens the message ready; YOU tap Send)

What it will NOT do on its own: send, post, pay, buy or delete anything, type
passwords or one-time codes, or unlock the phone. It stops and hands the phone back.
Typing is plain keyboard characters only (no accents / other scripts).

Switch Wireless debugging OFF when you are not using it.

PHONE  ->  PC  (any phone, including iPhone)
--------------------------------------------
Already built in: press the "Remote Control" button in Jarvis to show a QR code, then
scan it with the phone's camera (same Wi-Fi as the PC). You can then type or speak to Jarvis from the phone's
browser, and send files to the PC.

iPHONE: Apple does not allow a PC program to drive an iPhone, so only the
PHONE -> PC direction above works for it.
