OBSIDIAN AND COGNEE - where Jarvis keeps what you tell it
=========================================================
Run Install_Jarvis.bat again to add it (it keeps your key and memory).

WHAT CHANGED
  Spoken notes, and anything you ask Jarvis to remember, are now written into your Obsidian vault as ordinary
  Markdown notes instead of a text file. Obsidian is just a folder of .md files, so nothing is locked in: you can
  read, edit, search, link and sync them like any other note.
  Cognee (optional, off by default) can then read those notes and build a knowledge graph, so questions like
  "what did we decide about the launch?" are answered from your whole history, not just by matching words.

FIRST TIME
  Nothing to install for the Obsidian part. Jarvis uses your Obsidian vault if you have exactly one; otherwise it
  creates "Documents\Jarvis Vault" (in Obsidian choose "Open folder as vault" and pick it, once).
  To use a specific vault, say: "Use my vault at D:\Notes\Brain."
  Everything Jarvis writes goes in a folder called Jarvis inside the vault, so it never mixes with your own notes:
      Jarvis\Notes\2026-11-05 1423 - Spoken notes.md      a session you dictated, or something you asked it to remember
      Jarvis\Daily\2026-11-05.md                          one page per day, linking to that day's notes
      Jarvis\Memory.md                                    what Jarvis remembered before this feature (after "import my old memory")

JUST SAY
  "Remember that Asha prefers morning meetings."          "Remember this privately: ..."   (never sent to Cognee)
  "Take notes of everything I say."  ...  "Stop the notes."     (written live into a new Obsidian note)
  "Add to today's page that I called the lab."
  "What did I decide about the launch?"                   "What did Asha say last week?"
  "Find my notes on the incubator."                       "Search my whole vault for batteries."
  "Open my notes in Obsidian."   "Open today's page."     "Open the note about the launch."
  "Import my old memory."                                 "Set up my second brain."

COGNEE - OPT IN, AND WHAT IT COSTS
  Say "Set up my second brain with Cognee." Jarvis will tell you three things first, and only go ahead if you say yes:
    * It needs about 1.5 GB of disk and a few minutes to download. It lives in its own folder (second_brain\venv) so it
      cannot interfere with Jarvis. Removing it is deleting that folder.
    * It sends the TEXT OF YOUR NOTES to Gemini (the same service and key Jarvis already uses) so a language model can pick
      out people, projects, decisions and dates. Your questions are sent the same way. Cognee's own databases stay on this PC.
    * Your Obsidian notes work exactly the same without it.
  When a dictated session ends, the finished note is added to Cognee in the background. "Sync my notes" adds anything it
  has not seen; changed notes are re-sent, unchanged ones are not. "Turn Cognee off" stops all of it.
  "Clear the knowledge graph" asks you to confirm, and removes only what Cognee built. Your notes are never deleted.
  A note is never sent if it is private ("remember this privately", or a notes session you called private), or if you add
  the property  ingest: false  to it in Obsidian yourself.

IF COGNEE CAN'T ANSWER
  Jarvis says so and falls back to a keyword search of your notes, so you still get an answer.

GOOD TO KNOW
  * Building the graph is slow and uses your Gemini allowance: a long note can take a minute or more.
  * The first time Cognee runs it downloads a small tokenizer file, so it needs the internet.
  * The Gemini model names Cognee uses are in config\second_brain.json (llm_model, embedding_model). If Google retires
    one, change it there. Defaults: gemini/gemini-2.5-flash and gemini/gemini-embedding-001.
  * Old notes made as text files are in Documents\Jarvis Notes. They are not moved; drag them into the vault if you want them.
  * Searches look only in the Jarvis folder unless you say "my whole vault".

NOT TESTED HERE
  Everything is tested except Cognee building a graph through Gemini, which needs a live key and internet that the test
  environment did not have. If that step misbehaves, say "what's the second brain status?" and read out any error.
