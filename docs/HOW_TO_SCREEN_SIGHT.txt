SCREEN SIGHT - Jarvis can keep looking at your screen, until you say stop
=========================================================================
Run Install_Jarvis.bat again to add it (it keeps your key and memory).

JUST SAY
  "Keep an eye on my screen."          "You can see my screen now."
  "Watch my screen for the next hour." (it switches itself off after that)
  "Stop looking."                      "Turn off screen sight."
  "Never look at windows with 'payroll' in the title."
  "What's on my screen?" / "What does this error mean?"   (once it is on)

HOW YOU KNOW, AND HOW TO STOP IT
  A red badge stays on the window the whole time it is on: "SCREEN SIGHT ON - click to stop".
  Click it, say "stop looking", or just close Jarvis. It starts OFF every time Jarvis launches,
  unless you explicitly said "keep it on even after a restart".

WHAT IS SENT
  A small picture of your screen goes to Gemini (Google's service) every few seconds while the
  picture is changing, and now and then when it is not. Nothing is saved on your PC.
  It is paused while Jarvis is asleep (wake-word mode) or disconnected.
  Windows whose title looks private are skipped: password managers, private/incognito browsing,
  banking and sign-in pages, plus any words you add. A title is only a hint: a page can be
  sensitive without saying so, which is why the badge and the off switch matter most.

GOOD TO KNOW
  It looks at your main screen only. It uses more of your Gemini allowance while the screen is busy.
  While it is on, Jarvis does not comment unprompted; it answers when you ask.
  The "watch for something" rules in the automations feature are separate, and look on their own schedule.
