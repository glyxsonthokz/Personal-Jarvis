THE EARTH
=========
Run Install_Jarvis.bat again to add it (it keeps your key and memory). The first time you
open the Earth it downloads about 3 MB of imagery, so the globe appears a few seconds late.

JUST SAY
  "Show me the earth."                 "Switch to the holographic map."
  "Take me to Imphal."                 "Zoom in."   "Zoom out."   "Stop spinning."
  "Where is the ISS?"                  "When will the ISS pass over Delhi?"
  "Any earthquakes today?"             "Show flights here."   (zoom in to a region first)
  "How far is Delhi from London?"      (draws the route on the globe)
  "What time is it in Tokyo, and what's the weather there?"
  "Close the earth."

BY HAND
  Drag to spin or pan. Scroll wheel to zoom. Click anywhere for the local time and weather there.
  Double-click to fly in. Round buttons on the right: zoom, home, then layers (earthquakes,
  aircraft, satellites, day/night, place names), the map style, and close.

WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT
  * The night side, with city lights, is the real one, for this moment.
  * Earthquakes are the last week's magnitude 4.5 and above (USGS). The ISS and its orbit path are
    worked out on your PC from public orbit data (CelesTrak).
  * Aircraft come from the community ADS-B network adsb.lol: only planes within about 250 nautical
    miles of the centre of the view, and only when zoomed into a region. Coverage depends on volunteers.
  * Zoom in far enough and the globe becomes a flat satellite map (Esri World Imagery), or a dark map
    in the holographic style (CARTO).

NEEDS THE INTERNET for imagery (once), live data and map tiles. Offline you still get the globe if
it was opened before; the live layers show a note instead.
Map tiles come from Esri's and CARTO's public servers, as in the original demo: fine for personal use;
check their terms before using this for anything else.
