# Changelog

## 1.1.0
* Second brain: spoken notes and anything you ask Jarvis to remember are written to your **Obsidian** vault (Markdown with properties, daily pages and backlinks) instead of a text file.
* Optional **Cognee** knowledge graph over those notes, in its own environment, off until you ask, never fed private notes; falls back to keyword search of the vault.
* New `second_brain` tool: remember, daily, recall, find, open, sync, import_memory, forget_graph, setup, status.

## 1.0.0
Everything in this repository, as one release:
* Installer (`Install_Jarvis.bat`): installs under `%USERPROFILE%\Jarvis`, one desktop icon, brings settings across from an earlier `Mark-LVI` folder.
* Walkthroughs (`guide_me`): points at where to click, one step at a time, and types for you.
* Phone link (`phone_link`): uses an Android phone from the PC over ADB.
* Voice lock (`voice_lock`): responds only to the owner's voice.
* Automations: close apps on open, focus mode, continuous notes, screen watching rules.
* Orb HUD: a holographic globe look for the main window.
* Earth (`earth`): 3D globe, satellite maps, live earthquakes, ISS, aircraft, day/night, weather.
* Screen sight (`screen_sight`): keeps looking at the screen on command, with a red badge and a kill switch.
* Health check (`Diagnose_Jarvis.bat`) and a dashboard fix for changing networks.
